Leather Repair & Restoration

We offer professional leather repair and restoration services for all types of leather items including handbags, wallets, jackets, belts, shoes, furniture, and accessories. From everyday use items to luxury designer brands like Gucci, Louis Vuitton, Prada, Hermès, Coach, Michael Kors, and more — our experts carefully restore damaged leather, faded color, scratches, tears, and wear. We work with all leather types including full-grain, top-grain, nubuck, suede, patent leather, and exotic leathers. Available in every color with custom matching, dyeing, and refinishing to bring your valuable items back to life with a fresh, premium finish.

Handbag & Purse Repair

We repair all types of leather bags including ladies' handbags, clutches, tote bags, crossbody bags, satchels, travel bags, and designer purses. Our services include fixing torn handles, broken zippers, worn corners, scratched surfaces, peeling edges, loose stitching, and damaged lining. We specialize in luxury brand repairs for Louis Vuitton, Gucci, Prada, Coach, Michael Kors, and Kate Spade.

Whether your bag has color transfer stains, pen marks, water damage, or structural issues, we restore it to pristine condition. We replace hardware (buckles, chains, clasps), reinforce weak areas, and perform complete handle reconstruction. Our craftsmen match original stitching patterns and leather textures to maintain authenticity. Available for all colors including classic black, brown, tan, burgundy, navy, and custom color matching for unique shades.

Leather Jacket & Garment Restoration

Scratches, cracks, tears, scuffs, and faded spots on leather jackets, blazers, coats, vests, pants, and skirts are professionally restored to maintain their original style and strength. We repair biker jackets, bomber jackets, motorcycle jackets, vintage leather outerwear, and modern fashion jackets from brands like Schott, AllSaints, Zara, and custom-made pieces.

Our restoration includes tear patching, sleeve repair, zipper replacement, lining fixes, pocket repair, collar restoration, and hem adjustments. We handle all leather finishes — smooth, distressed, matte, glossy, and waxed. Color restoration services cover fading, sun damage, dye bleeding, and complete recoloring in black, brown, burgundy, navy, tan, grey, or any custom color. We also recondition dry, stiff leather to restore softness and flexibility.

Leather Shoe & Boot Repair

Complete repair services for leather shoes, boots, sandals, loafers, oxfords, and formal footwear. We fix scuffs, scratches, heel damage, sole separation, torn stitching, cracked leather, and worn-out areas on men's and women's leather footwear. Specializing in premium brands like Clarks, Dr. Martens, Red Wing, Allen Edmonds, Cole Haan, and designer shoes.

Services include heel replacement, sole stitching, elastic goring, zipper repair, buckle replacement, and full shoe reconditioning. We restore color to faded or scuffed leather shoes in any color — black, brown, tan, burgundy, navy, grey, or custom shades. Our shoe stretching and conditioning treatments revive stiff, dry leather while our patching repairs holes and tears invisibly. Perfect restoration for dress shoes, casual shoes, work boots, and luxury footwear.

Leather Color Refinishing & Dyeing

We fix color damage, fading, stains, discoloration, dye transfer, and sun bleaching with expert color matching and refinishing to make leather look brand new. Our dyeing process covers all leather items — bags, jackets, shoes, belts, wallets, and furniture. We match any color including classic shades (black, brown, tan, burgundy, navy) and custom colors (red, green, blue, pink, purple, grey, white, beige).

Professional color restoration services include full recoloring, touch-up dyeing, color change (change black to brown, etc.), edge painting, patina restoration, and protective finishing. We use premium leather dyes that penetrate deeply for long-lasting, fade-resistant results. Perfect for reviving old leather goods, covering stains and scuffs, or completely transforming the color of your leather items. Works on smooth leather, nubuck, suede, and patent leather with appropriate finishing treatments.

Leather Stitching & Patching

Strong stitching, lining repair, seam reinforcement, and professional patchwork solutions ensure durability for all leather brands — from regular use items to luxury designer products. We repair torn seams, burst stitching, frayed edges, ripped lining, and structural damage on bags, jackets, shoes, belts, wallets, and furniture upholstery.

Our stitching services use heavy-duty waxed thread in matching colors for invisible repairs or decorative contrast stitching. We perform hand-stitching for delicate work and machine-stitching for strength. Patching services include invisible patches for holes, tears, and burn marks using matching leather pieces. We also replace damaged panels, rebuild collapsed corners, and reinforce stress points. Available for all leather types including full-grain, top-grain, bonded leather, nubuck, and suede with color-matched thread in every shade.

Leather Furniture & Upholstery Repair

Complete repair and restoration services for leather sofas, chairs, recliners, ottomans, car seats, and upholstered furniture. Our leather conditioning treatments revive dry, cracked furniture while our protective coatings prevent future damage. Perfect for antique furniture restoration, office chair repair, car interior restoration, and luxury furniture maintenance.
