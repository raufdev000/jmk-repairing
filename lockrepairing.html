Lock Repair & Installation

Reliable lock repair and installation services for homes, offices, and shops. We ensure your security with professional tools and trusted workmanship. Whether you need traditional mechanical locks, modern digital locks, smart locks, or biometric systems, our expert locksmiths handle all types of locking mechanisms. We service and install locks from leading brands including Yale, Schlage, Kwikset, Godrej, Harrison, Dorset, Europa, and all major international and local manufacturers. From simple door locks to advanced access control systems, we provide comprehensive security solutions for complete peace of mind.

Door Lock Repair

Fix jammed, broken, or misaligned locks efficiently. Our experts diagnose and repair all types of door locks including cylinder locks, mortise locks, deadbolts, padlocks, and cam locks to restore smooth operation and security. We handle mechanical failures, key breakage extraction, lock cylinder replacement, and realignment issues.

Whether your lock is stuck, difficult to turn, or won't open/close properly, we provide on-site repair services for residential, commercial, and industrial locks. We work with all lock types from traditional pin tumbler locks to modern euro cylinder locks, ensuring quick resolution and minimal disruption to your daily routine.

New Lock Installation

Secure installation of modern and high-security locks. We install a wide range of locks including deadbolts, smart locks, digital keypad locks, biometric fingerprint locks, and multi-point locking systems. Our installation service covers main doors, bedroom doors, bathroom locks, office doors, and commercial premises.

We specialize in installing Yale smart locks, Godrej digital locks, August smart locks, Kwikset electronic locks, and traditional high-security locks from brands like Schlage and Medeco. Our technicians ensure proper alignment, secure mounting, and thorough testing of all locking mechanisms. We also provide key duplication and master key systems for businesses requiring multi-level access control.

Lock Replacement

Upgrade old or damaged locks for better protection. Replace worn-out locks with high-quality alternatives to enhance your property's security. We handle complete lock replacement for all types including mechanical locks, electronic locks, mortise locks, cylindrical locks, and rim locks with precision fitting and professional installation.

Our lock replacement service includes removing the old lock, preparing the door for new hardware, installing the replacement lock, and ensuring perfect operation. We recommend and install locks that match your security requirements and budget — from basic privacy locks to high-security Grade 1 deadbolts. Compatible with all door materials including wood, metal, UPVC, and glass doors.

Digital & Smart Lock Installation

Professional installation of digital and smart locks including keypad locks, fingerprint locks, RFID card locks, Bluetooth-enabled locks, and Wi-Fi connected smart locks. We install and configure popular smart lock systems like Yale Assure Lock, August Smart Lock, Schlage Encode, Godrej Advantis, and Samsung digital door locks.

Our smart lock service includes mobile app setup, user code programming, fingerprint enrollment, remote access configuration, and integration with home automation systems. We provide training on lock operation and troubleshoot connectivity issues. Perfect for tech-savvy homeowners and businesses seeking keyless entry convenience with enhanced security features like access logs and remote monitoring.

Security Upgrades & Advanced Systems

Improve safety with stronger and advanced locking systems. We recommend and install cutting-edge security solutions tailored to your needs including anti-theft locks, bump-proof locks, drill-resistant locks, and commercial-grade high-security locks. Our security assessment identifies vulnerabilities and suggests appropriate upgrades.
