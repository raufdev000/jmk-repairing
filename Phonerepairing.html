Phone Repairing

Fast, reliable, and affordable phone repair services for all smartphone brands. Whether you own an iPhone, Samsung Galaxy, or any Android device, our certified technicians handle screen replacements, battery issues, software fixes, water damage restoration, and more with professional precision. We specialize in repairing the latest flagship models including iPhone 16 Pro Max, Samsung Galaxy S24 Ultra, Google Pixel, OnePlus, Xiaomi, and all other popular brands.

iPhone Repair Specialists

Expert repair services for all iPhone models from iPhone 16 Pro Max, iPhone 15, iPhone 14 Pro, all the way back to iPhone 6 and older generations. We handle screen replacements, battery swaps, camera repairs, Face ID fixes, charging port issues, and back glass replacement.

Our technicians use genuine or high-quality aftermarket parts specifically designed for Apple devices. We ensure your iPhone is restored to perfect working condition with full functionality, maintaining iOS compatibility and Face ID/Touch ID features. All repairs come with a warranty for your peace of mind.

Samsung Galaxy Repair Experts

Professional repair services for all Samsung Galaxy models including the latest Galaxy S24 Ultra, S23 FE, Z Fold 5, Z Flip 5, A-series, and Note series. We repair AMOLED screens, replace batteries, fix charging issues, camera problems, and water damage on all Samsung devices.

Samsung's advanced technology requires specialized expertise — our team is trained to handle curved displays, under-display fingerprint sensors, fast charging circuits, and S Pen functionality. We use OEM-quality parts to ensure your Samsung Galaxy performs like new after repair.

Screen Replacement

Cracked or broken screens? We fix it professionally with high-quality LCD and OLED displays. Whether it's a shattered iPhone screen, Samsung AMOLED panel, or any other smartphone display, we replace it with precision-fitted glass that restores touch sensitivity and visual clarity.

Our screen replacement service includes thorough testing of touch response, display colors, brightness levels, and Face ID/fingerprint sensor functionality. We handle all screen types — from standard LCD to advanced LTPO AMOLED displays — ensuring perfect fit and original quality restoration.

Battery Replacement

Long-lasting batteries installed quickly and reliably. If your phone dies too quickly, overheats, or won't hold a charge, we replace it with a genuine or high-capacity battery that restores all-day battery life and fast charging capabilities.

We use premium batteries with proper voltage and mAh ratings matched to your phone model. After installation, we calibrate the battery and run diagnostics to ensure optimal charging speed, battery health reporting, and safe operation without overheating or swelling issues.

Software Fixes & Updates

Phone not working? We handle software troubleshooting efficiently. From boot loops and frozen screens to app crashes, iOS/Android update failures, and system errors, our technicians diagnose and resolve all software-related issues without data loss.

We perform OS reinstallation, firmware updates, virus/malware removal, iCloud/Google account unlocking, and system optimization. Whether your phone is stuck on the logo, won't turn on, or runs slowly, we restore full functionality through expert software repair and recovery.

Water Damage Repair

Quick and careful restoration for liquid-damaged phones. Dropped your phone in water, coffee, or any liquid? We use specialized ultrasonic cleaning and drying techniques to remove corrosion and moisture from internal components before permanent damage occurs.

Our water damage repair process includes immediate disassembly, motherboard cleaning, component-level micro-soldering if needed, and thorough testing of all functions. We recover phones that won't turn on, have display issues, or show charging problems after liquid exposure, saving your device and data whenever possible.
